# Tripo AI – aasaan tarika (step by step)

Game abhi poora chalta hai. Tripo se aap sirf cheezon ko **aur sundar** banate
hain. Aapko code ya settings kuch nahi chhoona: bas photo do, 3D file
download karo, aur mujhe bhej do. Baaki main laga dunga.

> Note: Tripo ki website maine khud kholkar nahi dekhi (wahan se mujhe access
> nahi mila), isliye buttons ke naam thode alag ho sakte hain. Kaam yahi hai.

---

## Kaam ek nazar mein

```
Photo kheencho  ->  Tripo mein daalo  ->  3D ban jaata hai  ->  GLB download  ->  mujhe bhejo
```

Ek baar mein **ek hi cheez**. Pehle sirf Shreenathji ki ek model bana kar
dekhiye; theek lage toh baaki.

---

## Step 1 – Photo kaise kheenchein

Achhi photo = achha 3D. Phone se hi kheenchiye:

1. **Ek photo mein ek hi cheez.** Kadhai ki photo mein sirf kadhai.
2. **Peeche saada background** – safed chadar, safed deewar ya kaagaz.
3. **Poori cheez frame mein**, chaaron taraf thodi jagah chhod kar. Kona kata
   na ho.
4. **Saamne se ya thoda tircha** (jaise khade ho kar mez par rakhi cheez dekhte
   hain). Bilkul upar se nahi.
5. **Din ki halki roshni**, khidki ke paas. Flash band. Tez parchhai nahi.
6. Shreenathji ki **chitraji** ho toh: seedha saamne se, sheesha ho toh
   reflection na aaye, poora swaroop dikhe.

**Photo nahi hai?** (jaise baithe hue Vaishnav) – toh Step 3 ka "likh kar
banao" tarika use kariye.

---

## Step 2 – Photo se 3D banana

1. Phone ya computer par **tripo3d.ai** kholiye aur login kariye (Google se ho
   jaata hai).
2. **"Image to 3D"** (ya "Image") chuniye.
3. Apni photo **upload** kariye.
4. Agar **"Smart Mesh"** / **"Clean topology"** / **"Low poly"** jaisa option
   dikhe, toh use chuniye – isse model halka banta hai aur phone par game
   tez chalta hai. **Quad** wala option **band** rakhiye.
5. **Generate** dabaiye aur ek-do minute ruk jaiye.
6. Ban jaane par model ko ungli se ghuma kar chaaron taraf se dekhiye. Theek na
   lage toh dobara generate kariye ya doosri photo try kariye.

## Step 3 – Bina photo ke: likh kar banana

1. **"Text to 3D"** (ya "Text") chuniye.
2. Neeche diya hua English vaakya **copy-paste** kariye.
3. Generate, phir upar jaise check kariye.

| Kya banana hai | Copy karke daaliye |
|---|---|
| Dada (dhoti-kurta) | A friendly elderly Gujarati grandfather sitting cross-legged on the floor, white dhoti and cream kurta, small white moustache, red tilak on forehead, tulsi bead necklace, hands resting on knees, cute stylized cartoon toddler-game character, big head, simple shapes |
| Ben (saree) | A smiling Indian woman sitting cross-legged on the floor in a bright pink saree with the pallu over her head, bindi, tulsi bead necklace, hands on knees, cute stylized cartoon character, big head, simple shapes |
| Bhai (kurta) | A cheerful Indian man sitting cross-legged on the floor in an orange kurta and white dhoti, red tilak on forehead, tulsi bead necklace, cute stylized cartoon character, big head, simple shapes |
| Bachcha | A happy Indian child sitting cross-legged on the floor in a yellow kurta, red tilak on forehead, cute stylized cartoon character, big head, simple shapes |
| Tokri | A shallow round woven bamboo basket, empty, light golden cane, thick rim, stylized cartoon, chunky toy-like proportions, single object |
| Bajot / chowki | A low Indian wooden bajot offering stool, four short turned legs, red lacquer top with a gold painted border, chunky stylized toy-like proportions, single object |
| Jhariji | A small brass Indian water pot with a round belly, narrow neck and a curved side spout, wrapped in a bright red cloth cover tied at the neck, stylized cartoon, single object |

Agar "negative prompt" ka khaana ho, toh usme daaliye:
`photorealistic, multiple objects, background, floor plane, text`

---

## Step 4 – Download kaise karein

1. **Download** / **Export** dabaiye.
2. Format mein **GLB** chuniye (FBX, OBJ nahi).
3. **Textures** (rang) ka option ho toh **on** rakhiye.
4. **Compress / Draco / Meshopt** jaisa kuch ho toh **off** rakhiye.
5. File ka naam aisa rakhiye ki pata chale kya hai: `shreenathji.glb`,
   `tokri.glb`, `dada.glb` …

---

## Step 5 – Mujhe kaise bhejein

Sabse aasaan: **Google Drive**.

1. Google Drive mein ek folder banaiye, naam: **Jalebi Seva models**.
2. Saari `.glb` files usme daal dijiye.
3. Mujhe yahin chat mein likhiye: *"Drive ke Jalebi Seva models folder mein
   files daal di hain."*

Main wahan se files leke game mein laga dunga, size aur jagah theek karke,
nayi APK bana ke bhej dunga.

(Chat mein seedha file attach ho jaaye toh woh bhi chalega.)

## Kis kram mein banayein

| Kram | Cheez | Kaise |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shreenathji** | Apni chitraji / sevya swaroop ki photo |
| 2 | **Tokri** | Ghar ki tokri ki photo, ya upar wala vaakya |
| 3 | **4 Vaishnav** (Dada, Ben, Bhai, Bachcha) | Upar wale vaakya |
| 4 | Kadhai, chaasni ka pateela, chulha | Ghar ke bartan ki photo |
| 5 | Pakhawaj, jhanjh, ghanti | Photo |
| 6 | Baaki (lota, katori, jhariji, bajot …) | Jab mann ho |

**Mat banaiye:** jalebi (woh game khud perfect banata hai), paatal, parda,
deewar – yeh code se hi achhe lagte hain.

---

## Aam dikkatein

| Dikkat | Kya karein |
|---|---|
| Model mein peeche ka saamaan bhi aa gaya | Saada background par dobara photo kheenchiye |
| Model ka ek hissa kata hua | Photo mein cheez ke chaaron taraf jagah chhodiye |
| Bahut kaala / parchhai wala | Din ki halki roshni mein, flash ke bina |
| Baithe hue Vaishnav ajeeb bane | Dobara generate kariye; 2–3 baar mein achha aa jaata hai |
| Download mein GLB nahi dikh raha | Jo mile woh bhej dijiye, main dekh lunga |
